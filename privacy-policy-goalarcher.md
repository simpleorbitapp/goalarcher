      <h3>GoalArcher Privacy Policy (English)</h3>
      <p><strong>Last updated:</strong> 3 November 2026</p>

      <p>
        PT NIAGA GLOBAL ULTIMATE provides GoalArcher — a financial goal calculator.
        This policy explains how the app handles data when you use calculators, save plans,
        create PDF reports, and view ads. For privacy questions, contact
        <a href="mailto:simple.orbit.app@gmail.com">simple.orbit.app@gmail.com</a>.
      </p>

      <h4>1. Data processed in the app</h4>
      <p>
        GoalArcher helps you estimate financial goals from the figures you enter.
        Input values, calculation results, and saved plans are processed on your device.
        GoalArcher does not require an account or login.
      </p>
      <p>
        The app does not intentionally send your financial-plan values, calculator inputs,
        or result figures to Google as ad targeting parameters or ad request values.
      </p>

      <h4>2. Local storage on your device</h4>
      <p>
        Unsaved values are used during the app session. Saved plans remain in the app’s storage
        on your device. GoalArcher also stores:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>your selected language,</li>
        <li>saved plans,</li>
        <li>and a flag showing whether the one free report export has already been used.</li>
      </ul>

      <h4>3. PDF reports</h4>
      <p>
        PDF reports are created on the device and saved to the location you choose through
        Android’s document picker. If you later share the file, the receiving app or service
        handles that copy under its own terms.
      </p>

      <h4>4. Ads</h4>
      <p>
        GoalArcher uses Google Mobile Ads to display native ads in selected information/content areas
        and rewarded ads to unlock additional report exports after the free export has been used.
        Ads require an internet connection.
      </p>
      <p>
        Google may collect or process IP address data (which may be used to estimate general location),
        app and ad interaction data, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as
        the advertising ID and App Set ID, depending on device settings, applicable rules, and your consent choices.
      </p>
      <p>
        GoalArcher does not send your financial figures in ad requests.
      </p>

      <h4>5. Ad consent and privacy options</h4>
      <p>
        Where required by region or applicable rules, the app shows Google’s consent form before certain ad types are shown.
        Your choices may affect whether ads are personalized or whether ads may be requested.
      </p>

      <h4>6. Data the app does not require</h4>
      <p>
        GoalArcher does not require your name, phone number, bank account, login, or user account.
      </p>

      <h4>7. Deleting data</h4>
      <p>
        You can delete plans individually or use the <strong>Delete Data</strong> feature to clear saved plans
        from the app. The flag showing that the free export has been used is not removed by Delete Data.
      </p>
      <p>
        Uninstalling the app deletes GoalArcher’s data stored on this device.
        Android app backup is turned off for GoalArcher, so your plans are not included in a Google account backup.
      </p>
